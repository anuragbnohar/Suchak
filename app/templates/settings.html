Removing an account stops it signing in — including the session it is holding right now — but keeps the person's name on the reviews they recorded. Those rulings are supervisory record and are not rewritten. You cannot remove your own account, nor the last super admin.

Role and entity can be changed here at any time, and take effect on that person's next click. Super admin is what carries the Overview tab and sight of every entity — pair it with guest below for somebody who should see the whole picture without being able to spend money or reshape anything. You cannot step your own account down, nor the last super admin.

A guest account is for somebody who should take part in the review work without being able to spend money on fetching or reshape the installation. They review, set aside and close to-dos exactly as their role allows. They cannot press anything that spends money — Fetch, Generate insights, Re-check alerts, Re-score — nor open Settings or Policy, manage people, or add, edit or remove an entity. It takes effect on their next click, not their next sign-in, and you cannot make your own account a guest — you would not be able to change it back.

Add an account

Add a second super admin before you rely on this application: one administrator who forgets a password is a locked-out system, and there is no reset link to fall back on.

Risk and severity definitions (the RBI guidance-note text the classifier follows) are edited on the Policy page. Your own password is changed on your account screen.
